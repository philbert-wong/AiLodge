Alerts

Leash messages your channel when an agent uses a trap key, tries to leak a key, or hits its limit, and when you stop every agent.

Where do I find a webhook address?

Slack: create an app at api.slack.com/apps, turn on Incoming Webhooks, add one for your channel and copy its address.

Discord: open the channel's settings, choose Integrations, then Webhooks, create one and copy its address.
